Frequently Asked Questions

Does it integrate with my existing clinic system?
Yes! BookMyAppointment syncs directly with Google Calendar and your cloud database with a 1-click authorization. There is no complicated software integration or technical background required.
How does the 6-Hour automatic slot release rule work?
If a patient books an appointment, the AI receptionist sends a confirmation request on WhatsApp 24 hours prior. If there is no reply, the AI calls the patient twice. If they remain unconfirmed 6 hours before the appointment, the slot is automatically freed and offered to patients on the waitlist.
Can the AI handle senior patients who use voice notes?
Absolutely. Our agent runs on Whisper v3. Patients can send voice notes in their regional dialect, and the AI will transcribe, understand, and reply back instantly with text.
Which languages are supported?
We support 12+ major Indian languages, including Hindi, English, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, and Assamese.
